# New Features & Pending Tasks (Stripe Migration)

## 🚀 New Features

### 👤 For Users (Ticket Buyers)

1.  **Simplified Checkout Experience**

    - **One-Page Payment:** No more 10-field billing forms. Removed requirements for address, phone number, billing city, and billing country.
    - **No ID/Cedula Required:** Payments are processed without requiring sensitive government ID documents.
    - **Modern UI:** Clean, responsive payment interface using Stripe's optimized Payment Element.

2.  **Enhanced Payment Options**

    - **Global Card Support:** Accept payments from international cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, etc.).
    - **Digital Wallets:** Support for Apple Pay and Google Pay (via Stripe Payment Element).
    - **3D Secure:** Built-in fraud protection with 3D Secure authentication support.
    - **Secure Redirect Handling:** RSVP details are stored server-side before 3DS redirect; client stores only a short-lived pending ID.

3.  **Transparent Pricing**
    - **Clear Fee Breakdown:** Checkout now explicitly displays the `Subtotal` and `Service Fee` separately, so users know exactly what they are paying for.
    - **Guaranteed RSVPs:** With the new "Auth & Capture" system, if an RSVP is approved, the ticket is guaranteed (funds are pre-authorized).

4.  **Promo Codes & Discounts**
    - **Discount Codes:** Apply promo codes directly at checkout to receive instant discounts.
    - **Clear Savings:** Discount amount is clearly displayed in the order summary and email receipt.

### 🏢 For Organizers

1.  **Direct Payouts & Financial Control**

    - **Stripe Express Dashboard:** Access to a dedicated dashboard to view earnings and upcoming payouts.
    - **Self-Serve Payouts:** Request payouts directly from the platform at any time (funds transferred to connected bank account).
    - **Automated Fee Handling:** Platform fees are automatically deducted at the time of transaction. Organizers never have to pay an invoice manually.

2.  **Improved RSVP Management**

    - **No Risk Approvals:** RSVPs now pre-authorize funds on the user's card. When you click "Approve", the payment is captured immediately. No more "ghosting" or declined cards after approval.
    - **Auto-Cancellation:** Unapproved RSVPs are automatically cancelled after 7 days, releasing the hold on the user's card.

3.  **Global Operations**
    - **International Sales:** Sell tickets to tourists and international visitors without payment friction.
    - **Real-Time Status:** View the connection status of your Stripe account directly in the organizer dashboard.

4.  **Promoter Tracking**
    - **Attribution:** Track sales generated by promoters via specific codes.
    - **Reporting:** Promoter codes are stored with every order for accurate commission calculation.
